BOG UI

Tab Bar

Open in Figma ↗

Bottom app navigation: the floating BOG tab bar (Light / Dark, 3–6 tabs, notification dots) with the real app icons, plus the flat Toolbar for iOS and Android with home indicator / system navigation. Pins to the bottom with data-position="fixed" or sits in the flow.

Usage

<!-- Floating tab bar (Tab Bar - iOS), pinned to the bottom of the page -->
<nav class="bog-tab-bar" data-position="fixed" aria-label="Main">

Try it

Tap the tabs: the selection pill slides, the icon switches to its filled version and tabbarchange fires. The bars use data-position="fixed"; inside a .bog-sheet-host phone frame they pin to the frame, on a real page to the viewport. Scroll the content to see the glass.
