Toggle Switch

Open in Figma ↗

An on / off switch for a setting that applies right away, on its own, as a setting row with a label and description, or in a group. On, off, indeterminate, error, read-only, disabled and skeleton; Themed and Inverse. For a choice that only applies after users tap Save, use a Checkbox.

Usage

<label class="bog-toggle">
  <span class="bog-toggle__row">
    <span class="bog-toggle__text">
      <span class="bog-toggle__title">Push notifications</span>
      <span class="bog-toggle__desc">Payments, transfers and offers</span>
    </span>
    <input type="checkbox" role="switch" class="bog-toggle__input" checked>
  </span>
</label>

<fieldset class="bog-toggle-group">
  <legend class="bog-toggle-group__label">Notifications</legend>
  …toggle rows…
  <p class="bog-toggle-group__message">You can change these any time</p>
</fieldset>

<!-- On its own (give it a name) -->
<input type="checkbox" role="switch" class="bog-toggle__input" aria-label="Contactless payments">

Try it

Tap anywhere on a row. The parent switch turns indeterminate when only some of its settings are on.

Motion

Switching on or off, the circle slides 20 px with a small overshoot past its spot and settles back. The track colour follows slightly later. Going in or out of Indeterminate, the circle squashes into the bar while it slides to the middle, overshoots by 1 px and settles. Source: Off ↔ On ↗ · Indeterminate ↗

A custom switch style. The circle and the track get their own animations, so each runs with its own curve and delay.

A composable switch with two animate*AsState values: the circle's offset and the track colour.

Built into toggle.css as CSS transitions. Nothing to call.
