BOG UI

Result Page

Open in Figma ↗

The full screen shown after a payment, transfer or request: a close button, the result in the middle and the next steps in the sticky bar. Type System uses Result Status (Success, Warning, Negative, Info, plus Pending while it's being processed); Type Custom shows any visual — like a bank card — instead of the status icon. Title, description, amount, action, feedback and a slot are optional.

Usage

<section class="bog-result-page" data-animate>   <!-- data-animate: play the load-in -->
  <header class="bog-result-page__nav">
    <button class="bog-icon-btn" data-type="neutral" data-size="small" aria-label="Close"><span class="bog-icon" data-icon="close"></span></button>
  </header>
  <div class="bog-result-page__main">
    <div class="bog-result" data-status="success">      <!-- success | warning | negative | info | pending | custom -->
      <span class="bog-result__icon"></span>
      <div class="bog-result__text">
        <h2 class="bog-result__title">Transfer sent</h2>
        <p class="bog-result__desc">Nino B. will get the money in a few seconds</p>
      </div>
      <div class="bog-result__amount">
        <p class="bog-result__amount-title">Amount</p>
        <div class="bog-amount" data-size="large" data-align="center"><div class="bog-amount__main"><span class="bog-amount__value">1,000.00</span><span class="bog-amount__currency">₾</span></div></div>
        <p class="bog-result__support">Fee 0.00 ₾</p>
      </div>
      <div class="bog-result__action"><button class="bog-btn" data-type="neutral" data-size="small">Save as template</button></div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <footer class="bog-result-page__bar">
    <p class="bog-result-page__helper">Message text</p>
    <button class="bog-btn" data-type="primary" data-width="fill">Done</button>
    <button class="bog-btn" data-type="neutral" data-width="fill">Share receipt</button>
  </footer>
</section>

<!-- Or play it yourself when the screen appears -->
<script>BOG.resultIn(document.querySelector(".bog-result-page"));</script>

Motion

The status appears first, then the text blocks fade in and rise into place one after another — the same step between blocks however many there are. Try every result in the phone on the right. Source: Result Pages ↗

One modifier for every block: it fades and rises with its own delay. The recipe gives each block its delay by its order.

A modifier with two Animatables per block (offset and alpha), started with the block's delay.

Built into bog-ui.js: BOG.resultIn(page), or data-animate on the page.
