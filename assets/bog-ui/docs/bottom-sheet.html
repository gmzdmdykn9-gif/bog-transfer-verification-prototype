BOG UI

Bottom Sheet

Open in Figma ↗

Slides up from the bottom over a dark overlay. The content area is a free slot: put any component inside. The header can have a title, description, both, or be blank. There's an optional sticky bar with a message and buttons at the bottom. Every sheet has an X close button by default (data-close="false" to hide it). You can also close it by tapping the overlay, pressing Escape, dragging the handle down, or with any data-sheet-close button.

Usage

<div class="bog-sheet" id="my-sheet" hidden>
  <div class="bog-sheet__panel" role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-labelledby="my-sheet-title">
    <header class="bog-sheet__nav">
      <div class="bog-sheet__heading">
        <h2 class="bog-sheet__title" id="my-sheet-title">Title</h2>
        <p class="bog-sheet__desc">Description</p>
      </div>
    </header>
    <div class="bog-sheet__content">
      …any content: text, inputs, lists, amounts…
    </div>
    <div class="bog-sheet__bar">
      <p class="bog-sheet__bar-text">Message text</p>
      <button class="bog-btn" data-type="primary" data-width="fill" data-sheet-close>Primary</button>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<button class="bog-btn" data-type="primary" data-sheet-open="my-sheet">Open sheet</button>
<!-- or from JS: BOG.openSheet("my-sheet"); BOG.closeSheet(); -->

Motion

The sheet rises from below the screen, overshoots its spot by 20 px and settles back; it closes with a quick accelerating drop while the overlay fades. Open every state from the buttons next to the phone on the right. Source: Chat Motion Guidelines › Action Sheet ↗

A presenter that keeps the sheet below the screen and plays the two-step rise. iOS 17+ (for the completion).

A host composable with one Animatable for the sheet's offset.

Built into bog-ui.js: every sheet opened with data-sheet-open or BOG.openSheet() plays it.
