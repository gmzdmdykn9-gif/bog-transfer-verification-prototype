Notification Badge

Open in Figma ↗

Count or dot that marks something new: single number, multi number (44+) and dot, in accent, negative, warning, positive and neutral. Sits on the corner of icon buttons, chips and avatars automatically.

Usage

<span class="bog-notification-badge">4</span>
<span class="bog-notification-badge" data-status="negative">44+</span>
<span class="bog-notification-badge"></span>                 <!-- dot -->

<button class="bog-icon-btn" data-type="neutral" aria-label="Notifications">
  <span class="bog-icon"></span><span class="bog-notification-badge">3</span>
</button>
