Feedback

Open in Figma ↗

Messages that tell the user what happened: Inline (inside the page) and Toast (floating, auto-closing). Positive, warning, negative, info and neutral; optional title, actions, close and loading; Themed and Inverse.

Usage

<div class="bog-feedback" data-status="positive" role="status">
  <div class="bog-feedback__text">
    <p class="bog-feedback__title">Payment sent</p>
    <p class="bog-feedback__desc">50.00 ₾ to Nino B.</p>
  </div>
  <button type="button" class="bog-feedback__close" aria-label="Close"></button>
  <div class="bog-feedback__actions">
    <button class="bog-btn" data-type="ghost" data-size="small">Details</button>
    <button class="bog-btn" data-type="primary" data-size="small">OK</button>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- Toast: floats over the screen and disappears after 4 s -->
<script>BOG.toast({ status: "positive", title: "Saved", text: "Template saved" });</script>
