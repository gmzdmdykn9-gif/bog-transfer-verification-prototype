BOG UI

Wizard Progress

Open in Figma ↗

Thin step bar under the navigation bar: done steps are full, the current step is half filled.

Usage

<div class="bog-wizard" role="progressbar" aria-valuemin="1" aria-valuemax="2" aria-valuenow="1">
  <span class="bog-wizard__step" data-state="current"></span>   <!-- done | current | (empty) -->
  <span class="bog-wizard__step"></span>
</div>

Variants

2 steps · Step 1
2 steps · Step 2
3 steps · Step 2
