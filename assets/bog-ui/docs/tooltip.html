BOG UI

Tooltip

Open in Figma ↗

A small dark label with an arrow that explains the element it points at. Tap the info icon (or any trigger) to open it; tap anywhere else, press Escape or tap the trigger again to close. 5 alignments × 2 positions.

Usage

<!-- trigger: the info icon (_Tooltip trigger), or any button / .bog-field__info -->
<button type="button" class="bog-tooltip-trigger" data-tooltip="tip-fee" aria-label="More info"></button>

<!-- the tooltip itself, anywhere in the page -->
<div class="bog-tooltip" id="tip-fee" role="tooltip" data-position="bottom" data-alignment="center">
  Fee is 1% of the amount
</div>

<!-- Text Input info button opens a tooltip the same way -->
<button type="button" class="bog-field__info" aria-label="More info" data-tooltip="tip-fee"></button>

<!-- shortcut, no tooltip element needed -->
<button class="bog-btn" data-type="secondary" data-size="small" data-tooltip-text="Copied to clipboard">Copy</button>

<!-- static (mockups, no JS): anchor + data-open -->
<span class="bog-tooltip-anchor">
  <span class="bog-tooltip-trigger"></span>
  <span class="bog-tooltip" role="tooltip" data-position="top" data-alignment="left" data-open>Tooltip</span>
</span>

On .bog-tooltip: data-position="bottom | top" (Figma Position), data-alignment="left | center | right | left-center | right-center" (Figma Alignment). left / right put the arrow 16px from that edge; left-center places the tooltip to the left of the trigger, right-center to the right (Position is ignored for those two). The tooltip flips and shifts to stay inside the .bog-sheet-host phone frame or the viewport, and the arrow keeps pointing at the trigger. Events on the tooltip: tooltipopen, tooltipclose (detail.trigger). JS: BOG.openTooltip(idOrEl, trigger), BOG.closeTooltip().

Try it

Tap the info icons. The ones near the edges flip or shift so the tooltip stays inside the phone frame.
